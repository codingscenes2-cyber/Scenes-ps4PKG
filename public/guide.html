The realm today

A journey with consequences.

Thirteen connected regions

Haunted forest, fallen capital, wetland, glacier, crystal undercity, volcano, glass desert, underground ocean, sacred ruins, living archive, eclipse fortress and the fractured seat. Discover roads, unlock sanctuary travel, read memorials and find hidden caches.

Tactical action combat

Read committed telegraphs, manage stamina, roll through danger, guard, parry and riposte. Heavy attacks break poise; guard counters reward timing. Inputs buffer briefly during recovery; dodge cancels open after an attack’s active hit.

Seven Soul Throne arcs

Defeat a throne guardian, recover its memorial and answer its witness. Return to its region to resolve its fate in Journal → Soul Thrones. Three irreversible choices per throne change testimony and the final age. Sealed revelations stay hidden.

A persistent wanderer

Eight starting classes, eight attributes, nine skill disciplines, sixteen weapon archetypes, forging, infusions, ten spells, four fixed difficulties, six testimony chapters and seven conditional conclusions. Earned progression survives death.

This is a compact procedural single-player RPG. Each region is an encounter space with its own roster and rules; it is not a seamless commercial-scale world. Dialogue is authored. No account is required.

Pause Escape Menu

Phone: drag the left stick; its outer edge sprints. Use the right action buttons and tap the interaction prompt. Hold Heavy for a charged follow-up. Expand the game to use the screen.

Controller menus: stick or D-pad up/down moves focus, A selects and B returns. Text entry uses keyboard or touch. The browser may require one controller button press before detection.

Combat openings and fairness
  • Attacks resolve after startup. Range and facing matter; a missed swing still costs stamina.
  • Inputs are buffered for 0.2 seconds. Dodges can cancel only the last 28% of attack recovery, after its hit has resolved.
  • Light armour rolls have 0.46 seconds of invulnerability; heavy armour has 0.30. Recovery is 0.60 seconds. These values never change with difficulty.
  • Watch an enemy’s red, amber or magenta telegraph. Cones have a safe rear, rings a safe center, marks commit to your former position.
  • A successful block opens a short guard counter. Repeated attacks can meet resistance, but never shorten a committed enemy windup.
  • Throne signatures can drain focus, seal flasks briefly, slow movement, feed roots or leave a second visible hazard. Their captions and HUD status explain the opening.
Steel · Veil · Soul

Power has a history.

Character → Disciplines opens three branches. Each root costs 120 essence, its next node 220 and its capstone 380. Skills survive death; increased reserves fill through rest or regeneration. Skill prerequisites and costs are enforced by the same pure rules used in tests.

Throne powers are earned after the guardian, memorial and witness. All three fates grant the same combat gift so narrative choices remain yours. The fate changes its epilogue, the final age’s coda, and—when boundaries are restored—the realm’s light and fog. Existing NPC consequences, including Crownfall’s extra flask or stronger guard, remain active.

Rarity describes a weapon’s origin, not hidden bonus damage. Attributes, requirements, upgrades and infusions determine performance. The deterministic balance model covers 128 class/difficulty/level combinations, limits combined skill/throne damage and defence bonuses, and reports stamina and hit-count budgets. It does not replace player testing.

Content editors: src/content/core.json contains classes, equipment, spells, region lore and roads; campaign.json testimony and dialogue; regions.json rosters, hazards, lighting, music and signatures; thrones.json choices; skills.json the skill graph; combat.json timing. Preserve region indices and save identifiers when extending an existing journey.

The ember endures

Play after the sandbox sleeps.

The game is hosted on GitHub Pages, independently of this development sandbox. Open the game online once and wait until Settings → Local save says Offline ready. Its service worker caches the game, lazy engine chunks, artwork, this guide and screenshots. Then reopen the same address without a connection. Supported mobile browsers can Add to Home Screen.

Autosave runs every two seconds and on page exit. Format-2 files contain a schema-1 character, timestamp and corruption checksum. Existing raw version-1 saves are accepted. If the primary snapshot fails validation, the last good copy loads. Settings reports recovery. Export a journey before clearing site data or changing devices. Private browsing and storage quotas can prevent persistence.

Death drops unspent essence at the fall location. A second death replaces the unrecovered drop. Levels, equipment, discoveries, decisions and defeated guardians persist. Difficulty stays fixed for that journey.

Export / Import in Settings transfers progress between browsers or origins. Import replaces the current local journey. The transport can support a future authenticated sync adapter; cloud synchronization is not implemented.
